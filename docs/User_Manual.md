# User Manual (صارفین کی رہنما کتاب)
**EduPulse DIK — Govt. Degree College No. 1, Dera Ismail Khan**

---

Yeh manual college ke tamam asateza, staff aur talba ke liye tayyar kiya gaya hai taake system ko ba-asani istemal kiya ja sake.

---

## 1. Login Kaise Karein (لاگ ان کرنے کا طریقہ)
1. Browser mein `public/index.html` ya `http://localhost:3000` open karein.
2. Apna Username aur Password (`Admin@123`) likhein.
3. Ya phir neeche diye gaye **"Quick 1-Click Viva Demo Roles"** mein se apne role par click karein.
4. Aap foran apne Dashboard par pohanch jayenge.

---

## 2. Teachers / Faculty ke Liye (اساتذہ کرام کے لیے ہدایات)

### حاضری لگانا (Daily Attendance Register):
1. Left sidebar se **"Attendance Register"** par click karein.
2. Apni class aur course select karein.
3. Agar sab bachay hazir hain to **"⚡ Mark All Present"** dabayein.
4. Kisi ghair hazir bachay ke samne **"A"** aur rukhsat wale ke samne **"L"** par click karein.
5. Upar **"💾 Save Attendance"** ka button dabayein.
*(Jin bachon ki hazri 75% se kam hogi, unke naam ke sath khud-ba-khud red warning badge lag jayega).*

### نمبرات درج کرنا (Marks Entry & Gradebook):
1. Sidebar se **"Marks & Gradebook"** open karein.
2. Har student ke samne **Sessional (out of 20)**, **Midterm (out of 30)**, aur **Final (out of 50)** ke number likhein.
3. Total marks, Gomal University ka letter grade (A+, A, B, etc.) aur GPA usi waqt khud calculate ho jayenge.
4. Sab number check karne ke baad **"💾 Save Marks"** dabayein.

---

## 3. Accounts Clerk ke Liye (اکاؤنٹس کلرک کے لیے ہدایات)

### جرمانہ وصول کرنا اور رسید بنانا (Fine Payment):
1. Sidebar se **"Fines & Payments"** par click karein.
2. Student ke samne green **"💵 Pay"** button dabayein.
3. Payment hotay hi status **PAID** ho jayega aur receipt generate ho jayegi.
4. **"🧾 Receipt"** button daba kar official slip print kar sakte hain.

### غلطی سے پے ہونے والا فائن ریورس کرنا ("Unpay / Reverse"):
1. Agar ghalti se kisi aur ka fine pay ho gaya ho to red **"↩️ Unpay"** button dabayein.
2. Waja (Justification Reason) likhein (maslan: "Wrong challan number entered").
3. System us payment ko reverse kar dega aur audit trail mein record save kar lega.

---

## 4. Students ke Liye (طلبہ کے لیے ہدایات)

### اپنا رزلٹ اور DMC پرنٹ کرنا:
1. Student portal par apna GPA aur attendance progress ring check karein.
2. Sidebar se **"Detailed Marks (DMC)"** par click karein.
3. Top right se **"🖨️ Print Official DMC"** ka button dabayein.
4. Official GDC No. 1 letterhead, stamps, controller signature aur QR code ke sath DMC print ho jayegi.
